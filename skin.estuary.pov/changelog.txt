[B]1.2.5[/B]
• A NEW install now starts with five main menu items: Movies, TV shows, TV,
  Add-ons and Weather. Music, Music videos, Radio, Pictures, Videos, Games and
  Favourites start switched off.
• None of those seven are gone. Every one of them is still listed, and switching
  any of them back on is one click, under Settings, Skin Settings, Main menu
  items.
• EXISTING boxes: if yours is updating from 1.2.4 the main menu you arranged is
  left exactly as it is and nothing changes. If yours is updating from 1.2.3 or
  earlier straight to this release, its main menu WILL be set to those same five
  items, because 1.2.4 is the release that marks a box as already arranged and
  yours never ran it. Switch the ones you want back on in Skin Settings.
• The seed runs once per profile and is stamped afterwards, so it can never
  rearrange a menu a second time. The seven Skin.SetBool lines run before the
  stamp on purpose: if a load is cut short part way through, the stamp is not
  written and the whole set is reapplied on the next Home load rather than
  leaving a half applied menu.

[B]1.2.4[/B]
• Skin settings no longer show rows that cannot do anything. Switch a home menu
  item off and its "Edit widgets" and "Default select action" rows disappear
  rather than sitting there greyed out. Movies, TV shows, Music, Music videos,
  TV, Radio and Add-ons all behave this way.
• Turn off "Show media fanart as background" and the three fanart pack pickers
  go with it. Turn off the video OSD autoclose and its seconds row goes too.
• "Choose genre fanart pack" was worse than greyed out. With fanart switched off
  it stayed fully clickable and picking a pack did nothing at all. It now hides
  alongside the other two pickers.
• The virtual keyboard suggests words as you type. Kodi installs the
  AutoCompletion add-on together with the skin, on an update as well as on a
  fresh install, so the suggestion strip above the keyboard is no longer
  permanently empty.
• Groundwork with no visible effect: marks profiles that already run the skin as
  arranged by their owner, so that a later release can lay out a default home
  menu on brand new installs only.

[B]1.2.3[/B]
• "Number of items to show in home screen lists" now applies to every home row.
  The Movies and TV shows poster rows already followed it. The Music, Music
  videos, Add-ons, Games, TV and Radio rows were fixed at 15, and no value of
  the setting could move them.
• The live TV and radio rows that carried no limit at all were rendering their
  whole directory rather than a row. Measured on a Fire TV with the setting at
  5: the TV channel groups row held all 12 groups while the poster rows held 5.
• The random music video artists row was pinned to 10 and now follows the
  setting like the rest.
• The Categories rows stay at 20 on purpose. They are navigation rather than
  content, so shrinking them would remove entries such as Playlists and Add-ons.
• The setting takes effect without restarting Kodi, a few seconds after the row
  is next on screen. The button itself is unchanged: one press cycles 5, 10, 15,
  20, 25, and left and right step and stop at the ends.

[B]1.2.2[/B]
• Apple TV only: writes a stand-in for the _scproxy module Kodi 22's tvOS build
  does not ship. Without it urllib.request cannot be imported, so every add-on
  whose dependencies include script.module.requests dies at startup. Measured on
  atv1: POV, Multi Weather, script.openweathermap.maps and EZ Maintenance++ all
  declare it. The shim goes in that dependency's own lib directory, which Kodi
  already puts on the path of everything that declares it, and is rewritten on
  any start where its contents differ.
• Apple TV only: Siri remote keymap. Back leaves fullscreen video with playback
  continuing, double play/pause returns to it, back at Home no longer opens the
  Favourites browser, and select opens the OSD on live TV.
• No change on Fire TV, Android or desktop: both writes are gated on tvOS.
